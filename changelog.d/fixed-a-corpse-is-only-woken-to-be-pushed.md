- **Walking over a body disturbs it, and walking near one no longer disturbs the pile.** A shove was
  refused outright below a metre a second — under half of walking pace — so crossing a corpse slowly
  passed through it with nothing happening at all. It is proportional the whole way down now, lift
  included, so a body crossed at a crawl is nudged and one crossed at a run is thrown.
  Separately, the field admitted any body whose **hips** were within 1.6 m and woke it before asking
  whether there was a limb within 0.8 m to push — and a man lying down is two metres one way and
  half a metre the other, so a sphere about his hips reaches a metre into empty sand across him.
  Bodies nobody was standing on woke, pushed nothing, and settled again, each one putting a skeleton
  back in the tree that `Corpse` exists to keep out of it.
