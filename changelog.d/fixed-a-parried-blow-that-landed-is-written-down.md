- **A parry that missed is a hit taken, and now the body falls like it.** Only the perfect window
  negates a blow; late halves it and anything past that takes it whole — but every hit that arrived
  during a parry was handed to the parry and forgotten, so nothing wrote down where it came from or
  how hard. A killing blow parried a beat too late threw the body by whatever hit it last instead:
  a jab from the other side of the wave, or on a run where everything else had been parried, by
  nothing at all — a man who crumples where he stands after taking a club to the head. The same
  silence meant a fully missed parry shook the camera not at all, having cost full health. A late
  parry also writes the blow down as what it became rather than what it was thrown as: it takes the
  weight out as well as the damage. Held by `tools/verify_combat.tscn`.
