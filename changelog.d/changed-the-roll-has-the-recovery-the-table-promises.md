- **A roll can no longer cover its own recovery.** The invulnerability opens a beat into the roll
  and closes before the roll does, so its last fifth of a second is exposed on purpose — but rolling
  again the instant one ended opened the next set of frames exactly over that window, and the
  weakness could never be met. A player with breath was untouchable for more than half of every
  second. The short cooldown the balance table has always described is now implemented, so between
  two rolls there is a gap about as long as a farmer's committed swing. It is not a second gate on
  stamina: a perfect parry refunds more than it costs, and without this that reward bought
  uninterrupted invulnerability — the parry funding the thing that made the parry unnecessary.
