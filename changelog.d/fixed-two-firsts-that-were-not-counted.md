- **The first blow of a run costs the flawless bonus, and one flare no longer recolours the rest.**
  The wave director measures the bonus by watching health fall, and had nothing to compare the very
  first fall against — the event that would have primed it is emitted while the player is still
  readying its own children, before the director exists to hear it. So the one hit every player takes
  was swallowed, and a wave they were hit in still paid thirty per cent extra. Separately, the eight
  pooled impact effects all pointed at a single flare material: a perfect hit anywhere recoloured
  every flare on the island, and one fading out dragged the others down with it. The coconut carried
  the fix for this already; the impact did not.
