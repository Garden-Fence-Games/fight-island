- **The big glowing farmer is a runner, not a harder fight.** It stands where it arrived, notices
  the player from closer than anyone, and then runs away from them — slower than the player walks,
  so it can always be caught — and it never swings. Catch it and it throws 20 coins and 15 rounds.
  Reach the sea and it slips under with all of it; the end of the wave takes it too. Exactly one
  comes on wave 3, and from wave 4 each body has a flat one-in-ten chance of being one.
