- **The gun and the stick can be picked out of the sand.** Both carry their own light now — an
  emissive body with a lit rim and a small lamp spilling onto the ground, breathing together — and
  the stick is twice the bar it was, nine centimetres across having been about one fat pixel at the
  distance the camera is fixed at. They landed in shot every time and were walked past anyway, which
  is the same thing that happened to the coconut and is fixed here the same way. The gun glows in
  **its own paint** rather than under a wash: the rig's texture becomes the emission, so what lifts
  off the sand is the weapon the artist drew.
